2aFizz · chosen, with ‽ and emoji

Fizz (1a) is the game face. It now has the interrobang and a 24-glyph emoji set drawn on a 7 × 7 grid that sits on the cap height. Emoji are single-colour, so they take the text colour or any EGA tint, and the bold, oblique and condensed styles leave them unchanged. A colour version can come later.

Pixel fonts · five candidates

Inspired by DOS and older games, but built to stay clean: open counters, a real lowercase and even spacing. Every glyph is a pixel grid, the same format as the game's sprites, drawn here at whole-number scales. The four styles (bold, oblique, condensed, outline) and the monospace setting are generated from each grid, so the family stays consistent. All five share one master drawing (Fizz), which means traits can be mixed later, e.g. Billy's proportions with Zargoth's corners. The winner can be exported as a TTF or WOFF2 font from the same grids.

Covers A–Z, a–z, 0–9, punctuation, Canadian French accents, curly quotes, em dash, middot, guillemets, arrows, box drawing, and button and keycap glyphs written as {A} or {Esc}.

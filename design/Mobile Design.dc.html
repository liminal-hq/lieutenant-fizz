Lieutenant Fizz · Android · landscape · turn 2

Glass pad, in the Fizz font

Iterating on 1a: the HUD numbers and button labels now use the Fizz pixel font, with snacks in yellow and fizz in cyan to match the desktop scoreboard. The glass stays. Each option tries a different control layout.

2a
D-pad + triangle
Haptic · {{ d.haptic }}

The 1a layout with the new type. This is the baseline: one round pad, plus Jump, Pogo and Fizz in a triangle.

2b
Split arrows + rising row
Haptic · {{ e.haptic }}

Big separate ◀ ▶ keys, so running never turns into an accidental aim. ▲ and ▼ get their own small column to aim the Fizz Blaster. The action buttons rise toward the corner, Fizz → Pogo → Jump, so the thumb rolls up the row.

2c
Two buttons + pogo switch
Haptic · {{ f.haptic }}

Pogo is a mode, not an action, so it becomes a pill switch above the thumb and stays out of the way. That leaves two large buttons, Jump and Fizz, which are much easier to hit without looking.

Lieutenant Fizz · Android · landscape · turn 1

In-level HUD and touch controls

Three directions, all playable. Tap or hold the on-screen controls; with the mouse over a phone you can also use ← → move, ↑ ↓ aim, Z jump, X pogo, C fizz and P pause. Pick one and I'll build the other seven screens in that style. The D-pad and the floating joystick will both be options in Settings.

1a
Glass pad
Recommended
Haptic · {{ a.haptic }}

Afterglow glass D-pad, with three round buttons in a triangle under the right thumb. Jump is the largest and sits where the thumb rests. ↑ and ↓ on the pad aim the Fizz Blaster, as they do on the keyboard. The HUD is three glass pills, and pause is always visible. This maps 1:1 to the gamepad, so the help text works for both.

1b
Pixel joystick
Haptic · {{ b.haptic }}

Floating joystick: touch anywhere on the left half to plant it, then slide up or down to aim. The buttons are EGA plates in the pixel font, laid out in a row like Keen's keys, and the HUD is a Keen-style scoreboard. The overlay reads as part of the game rather than sitting on top of it.

1c
Thumb arc
Haptic · {{ c.haptic }}

Two tall move zones, so there's no stick to find. Jump, Pogo and Fizz sit on an arc the right thumb can reach without lifting. Slide up off Fizz to fire upward. The HUD stays hidden until a number changes, then fades out again. This is the calmest screen and the best one-handed reach.

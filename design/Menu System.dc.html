Fizz title · built on 2a, fills the viewport

No fixed 320 × 200 canvas. Like the shipped game, the scene is drawn at native resolution, about 12 tiles tall at a whole-number pixel scale, and the menu sits in the DOM layer on top. Type size steps between 2×, 3× and 4× with the viewport height, padding follows the shipped clamp() rules, and on narrow screens the logo breaks onto two lines. Behind the menu, attract mode pans back and forth across the real Crater Fields, Crystal Caves and Mildred’s Citadel layouts, then fades to the next level. The bullet is a can of fizz.

Every frame is the full prototype: New Game plays the scene with the HUD, Esc pauses, and Load, Save, Options and Controls all work. Text size Large in Options steps the type up a size. Open it full screen →

Fizz menu systems · four directions, eight screens

DOS shareware feel, modern behaviour. All text is set in the Fizz pixel font, and the backdrops and cursors are the game’s own sprites. Every frame is live: hover or click rows, or hover a frame and use the arrow keys and Enter (left and right change Options values). Hints switch to gamepad glyphs when a pad connects. Ben idles, leans on the logo, pogos across it, looks at the menu when the selection moves, and waves when you pick something. Reduced motion freezes the animation and shows card text in full.

Tweaks: force keyboard or gamepad hints, and preview reduced motion.

Menu system · three directions

Each option shows the title screen, the pause menu and a card (game over) at 640 × 400. Items and copy come from episodes/episode-1/src/game.ts; colours stay inside the 16-colour EGA palette from packages/engine/src/palette.ts plus the Afterglow overlay tokens. All three are DOM overlay at native resolution, so text stays crisp over the pixel canvas.

1aAfterglow overlay

Evolves what ships today: unboxed Space Grotesk list over a soft scrim. Adds a full-row highlight, grouped pause items, value chips and a reason on disabled rows.

The story

Ben Blaze is ten, lives for tinkering and has never once left his street. Then his cousin Billy goes missing, and a map in Billy’s handwriting points off the planet.

The Zargs of Planet Zargoth have been stealing Earth’s cocoa beans to refill their drying chocolate rivers. Their boss, Mildred McMire, has bigger plans: every river is being poured into the galaxy’s largest chocolate castle, and she intends to live in it.

Ben follows the map in a spaghetti-and-meatballs saucer, with a pogo stick for high ledges and a Fizz Blaster that fires cream soda bubbles. The bubbles stun. They never harm.

C:\FIZZ\GUIDE> type CAST.TXT

The cast

  • Ben Blaze, “Lieutenant Fizz”

    A ten-year-old neighbourhood tinkerer in a bicycle helmet. Armed with a modified pogo stick and the Fizz Blaster, which stuns instead of harming.

  • Billy

    Ben’s cousin, fourteen. He went after the Zargs and now sits in a cage at the heart of the citadel.

  • Mildred McMire

    Ten years old and the Zargs’ boss. She is turning every chocolate river into the galaxy’s largest chocolate castle, and pilots the Cocoa Colossus from its glass dome.

  • The saucer

    A spaghetti-and-meatballs flying saucer with a noodle seat and meatball thrusters, found in a secret lab under the treehouse. Parked where Ben lands.

Snacks and scoring

  • Cheezies1 point
  • Choc bar2 points
  • Fudge cookie5 points
  • Cream soda+5 fizz shots
  • Red gumdropOpens red cookie doors
  • Blue gumdropOpens blue cookie doors
  • Green gumdropOpens green cookie doors
  • Gold USB driveDropped by the boss; hacks the terminal

You start with 3 lives and earn another for every 100 points. Losing a life returns you to the map. Press F5 to save and F9 to load, or use the pause menu: there are four slots plus a read-only autosave.

C:\FIZZ\GUIDE> type WORLD.TXT

The world

Fifteen levels sit on a top-down map of crystal forest split by chocolate rivers. Teleporters link the regions and power up as you clear levels. The map saves itself every time you visit it.

  • CRATER FIELDS <DIR> south-west, where Ben lands

    • ├─Crater Fields. Slopes, pogo, fizz and chocolate pools. The tutorial.
    • ├─Meteor Mesa. Daylight mesas, clouds and a hover platform.
    • └─Zarg Lookout. A nine-floor watchtower. Climb to the roof for the red key, then come back down.

  • MARSHMALLOW MEADOWS <DIR> north-west

    • ├─Marshmallow Meadows. Soft hills and corrals of bouncing marshmallows.
    • ├─Bonbon Playhouse. A candy theatre with rooms hidden behind painted flats.
    • └─Fudge Bog. Optional. Wide fudge pools, spike runs and spore pods.

  • ROCK CANDY REACH <DIR> north-east

    • ├─Crystal Caves. Dark tunnels lit by crystals and your lantern.
    • ├─Mirror Shafts. Bounce fizz off mirrors to wake the crystal switches.
    • ├─Whisper Hollow. Optional. Vines, chimneys and wall-kicks.
    • └─Sugar Glass Gallery. Optional. A long theatre of hidden rooms, and a secret.

  • FROSTING FRONTIER <DIR> south-east

    • ├─Frosting Flats. Optional. The hardest open-sky level.
    • ├─Frosting Spire. Twelve floors, three lifts and a key in every colour.
    • ├─Cocoa Foundry. Conveyor belts, crushing presses and molten metal.
    • └─Mildred’s Citadel. Boulder ramp, sentries, phantoms, then the boss and Billy’s cage.

  • GUMDROP ISLE <DIR> a secret island in the lake

    • └─Gumdrop Isle. A short bonus course, packed with snacks. Find the way in.

C:\FIZZ\GUIDE> type MOVES.TXT

Moves and tricks

  • >JUMP Release early for a short hop.
  • >POGO Toggle it on and Ben bounces by himself. Hold jump for a high bounce, high enough for the ledges about seven tiles up that hold a cookie and two cheezies. Stomping while pogoing bounces higher still.
  • >FIZZ Hold ↑ to aim up, or ↓ in the air to aim down. Cans of cream soda refill your ammunition.
  • >WALL-KICK Press jump against a wall in mid air. Two facing walls, at least three tiles apart, make a climb.
  • >MANTLE Just miss a ledge by about a tile and Ben hauls himself up.
  • >LADDERS AND VINES Press ↑ to grab on, jump to let go. You can shoot while climbing.
  • >CRACKED WALLS Shoot one and it crumbles. A crack is always a promise, never a trap.
  • >MIRRORS A bubble bounces off a 45-degree mirror. Use that to reach switches you cannot see directly.
  • >PAINTINGS Touch one for a line of lore. Some of them point at secrets.

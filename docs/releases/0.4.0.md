**Lieutenant Fizz 0.4.0** adds the opening titles, which play before the title menu and a new game, and gives quick-save a slot of its own.

## Downloads

- **Linux** (x86_64 and aarch64): `.deb`, `.rpm` and AppImage. The packages are unsigned.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon and Intel): one universal `.dmg`. It is not notarised, so macOS blocks the first open; see below.
- **Windows** (x64 and arm64): an `.msi` and a setup `.exe`. They are unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on first run; see below.
- **Android:** a signed universal APK (minSdk 26, landscape) and the signed AAB for Google Play. The mapping file and the native debug symbols are attached for reading crash reports.
- **`SHA256SUMS`** covers every file. Check your download against it.

The [getting-started guide](https://github.com/liminal-hq/lieutenant-fizz/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) has the steps for each system.

## What is new

- **The opening titles.** Ben runs along a chase with the Zargs behind him, firing fizz bubbles that stun the ones they hit, to a score of its own, then the picture and the sound fade out together into the title menu. They follow the DOS game's rules for when they play: before the title menu, and before the story on a new game (the launcher's New game goes straight from the titles to the story, with no title screen between). Back skips them, and a skip needs a release of the key or button first, so the press that skips them does not carry on into the next screen.
- **Quick-save has a slot of its own.** F5 saves to a quick-save slot that the Load game screen lists and the Save game screen does not, so it no longer takes one of the four manual slots. The launcher's Load game offers it too (`launch=load:quick`).
- **A fix in the opening cinematic.** Ben now climbs down the lab ladder with the right frames.
- **Smaller changes.** The AppImage notes say it bundles the GStreamer audio plugins.

## Known limits

- The Linux, macOS and Windows builds are not signed by a publisher or notarised.
- Controller rumble has been tried with a DualShock 3 and a DualShock 4 on Linux only. It is untried on macOS, Windows, Xbox pads and DualSense.
- Most levels have been proved finishable by a playtest bot but not yet played through by people, and the boss fight is untested; difficulty tuning is still open.

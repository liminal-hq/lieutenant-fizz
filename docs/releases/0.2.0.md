**Lieutenant Fizz 0.2.0** adds Enhanced music and Enhanced sound effects, each chosen from the Sound screen.

## Downloads

- **Linux** (x86_64 and aarch64): `.deb`, `.rpm` and AppImage. The packages are unsigned.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon and Intel): one universal `.dmg`. It is not notarised, so macOS blocks the first open; see below.
- **Windows** (x64 and arm64): an `.msi` and a setup `.exe`. They are unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on first run; see below.
- **Android:** a signed universal APK (minSdk 26, landscape) and the signed AAB for Google Play. The mapping file and the native debug symbols are attached for reading crash reports.
- **`SHA256SUMS`** covers every file. Check your download against it.

The [getting-started guide](https://github.com/liminal-hq/lieutenant-fizz/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) has the steps for each system.

## What is new

- **Enhanced music:** Options > Sound > Music version switches the level, overworld and boss tracks to songs of about a minute. Each is written from the track's own notes, with an intro, a contrast section in a related key, a breakdown and build, and a final chorus doubled an octave up, and it loops back to its start without a seam. Every melodic part also gets a filter that opens at each note's attack and a soft partner voice on the other side of the stereo field, which gives the notes some depth. The title, cinematics and ending keep their original music.
- **Enhanced sound effects:** Options > Sound > Effects version switches to effects built with tricks borrowed from old AdLib sound chips: sweeps that rise, fall and wobble, a filter that opens at each attack, and a short bell-like glint on the key, USB stick, extra life and level-clear jingles. Each effect keeps about the same length as the original.
- **Classic is still the default.** Both rows start on Auto, which plays the original music and effects, and stepping a row saves your choice. Reset on the Sound screen puts both back on Auto. The rows are separate from Style, which places sound in the stereo field.

## Known limits

- Enhanced music and effects were tuned from measurements and not yet widely listened to; the level balance and the loop points are the first things to check by ear.
- The Sound screen has eight rows now, so on a short phone screen it scrolls.
- The Linux, macOS and Windows builds are not signed by a publisher or notarised.
- Controller rumble has been tried with a DualShock 3 and a DualShock 4 on Linux only. It is untried on macOS, Windows, Xbox pads and DualSense.
- The macOS and Windows installers have been built but not yet opened on a Mac or a Windows PC.
- The app opens on a simple menu first (Episode 1 and some test pages); the launcher is still to come.
- Most levels have been proved finishable by a playtest bot but not yet played through by people, and the boss fight is untested; difficulty tuning is still open.
- A set of phone-width browser tests is switched off while its flakiness is reviewed (see `docs/STATUS.md`).

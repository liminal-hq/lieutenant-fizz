## Answers to the two questions

**What are the enemy stun recovery times?** (`world.rs`)

| How | Stun |
|---|---|
| Stomp (`world.rs:1586`) | 3.0 s |
| Fizz shot (`world.rs:2466`) | 6.0 s |
| Beetle after charging into a wall (`world.rs:2026`) | 1.2 s |

A shot on an enemy that is already stunned passes through it and does not refresh the timer (`world.rs:2435`). A stomp on a stunned enemy bounces Ben and does not refresh it either (`world.rs:1573`).

**Do stunned enemies resume their loop or start over?** They resume. `ai()` returns early while `stun > 0` (`world.rs:1968`), so the enemy's state, direction and cooldown timers are frozen where they were (a gravity step still runs so they land). A stomp leaves `state` alone, so a charging Beetle that is stomped goes back to charging; a shot sets `state = Idle`, so a shot enemy starts its next behaviour fresh. Flyers other than the Bat hang in place. Nothing plays when the stun ends, which is the root of "enemy animation coming out of stun" and "stunned shooters should respawn their shot like the flower's puff": the shot timers freeze, then fire on the first tick after thaw.

## Checked in the code — likely causes

- **Grabbing a vine going down doesn't work.** `tick_climb` (`world.rs:1748`) only grabs a vine on Up. Down is handled by `from_top`, which tests `has_ladder` and never `has_climb`, so standing on a ledge above a vine and pressing Down does nothing. Fix: let `from_top` also accept a vine under Ben's feet.
- **Foundry crushers you can jump through.** `Kind::Press` is a prop with no solid body: contact is lethal only while it is more than `PRESS_DANGER_DROP` (1.6 tiles) below its rest height (`world.rs:1547`), and it kills rather than blocks. A raised press is walk-through and a lowered one is a hitbox, so Ben can jump up through the gap. Needs a design call: make the head solid while it descends, or keep it lethal-only and make the telegraph clearer.
- **Phantom fires before and after teleporting.** `teleport_phantom` (`world.rs:2521`) pushes a white `Fx` at the old position and a `Poof` caption at the new one in the same tick, then sets `alpha = 0` and `fire_t = min(fire_t, 0.8)`. Both the vanish and arrival effects read as fires; the arrival one has no delay before the phantom can attack. This matches "needs a spawn fire and a respawn fire delay".
- **Retry level button.** 342 Foundry attempts in 16 minutes, and 181 deaths across the 196 attempts in the long sample, show how often the restart loop runs. The death card and pause menu are where the button would go. Not yet looked at in the shell.

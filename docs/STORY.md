# Story Text

## Opening cinematic
1. One night, Ben Blaze's cousin Billy didn't come home. Under the treehouse where they always played, Ben found a secret lab, a prototype spaghetti with meatballs flying saucer, and a note Billy had left behind: a map, with an X and a planet name beside it. "That must be where he's gone." Ben put on his bicycle helmet, hit the Engage button, and was off.
2. The G-force pressed Ben deep into the oversized, noodle-upholstered captain's chair as the saucer tore through the stratosphere. Marinara-scented exhaust plumed behind him. He gripped the steering yoke, which felt suspiciously like a giant, hardened breadstick.
3. On the dashboard, pinned beneath a blinking, meatball-shaped radar dial, was Billy's note. The destination was boldly scribbled: Planet Zargoth, a remote outpost on the outer galactic rim.
4. Ben engaged the autopilot and popped open the glovebox: a crinkled bag of Cheezies and a cold can of Canadian cream soda. The crunch echoed in the quiet cabin as the stars stretched into streaks of white light.
5. Hours later, the navigation console chimed a retro 8-bit melody. Planet Zargoth loomed through the windshield, a swirling marble of neon green and deep purple clouds.
6. He touched down with a heavy, saucy thud in a glowing, crystalline forest. Ben adjusted his chin strap, tucked his pogo stick under one arm, and stepped out to find his cousin.

## Boss intro
- **Mildred:** Ben Blaze? Mortimer said Billy's little cousin might show up. Cute helmet.
- **Mildred:** Every cocoa bean on Earth is going into my castle, the biggest chocolate castle in the galaxy! Colossus, squash him!
- **Ben:** Not before I get Billy back.

## Boss defeated
- **Mildred:** My Colossus! Fine, keep your silly cousin. I've got a castle to finish!
- **Ben:** It dropped something… a gold USB drive. There's a security terminal back by the entrance.

## Ending
1. The gold USB drive slid into the security terminal with a click. Green text raced up the screen, and Mildred's security system gave up.
2. The cage door swung open. Billy straightened his football helmet and grinned. "Took you long enough, Lieutenant Fizz."
3. Mildred's voice echoed down the chocolate halls: "This isn't over, Ben Blaze! Mortimer and I have plenty more castles to build!"
4. The cousins raced back to the saucer, its hold stuffed with every cocoa bean the Zargs had taken. Earth's chocolate was safe, for now.
